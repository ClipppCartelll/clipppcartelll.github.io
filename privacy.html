Privacy Policy — Shorts Robot

Shorts Robot is a personal tool used only by the owner of a YouTube channel to upload Shorts to their own channel.

What it accesses

Permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. Nothing else.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not shared, sold, or sent anywhere except to Google to upload videos.

Other people's data

The tool does not collect, store, or share any data about other people.

Contact

Use the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
